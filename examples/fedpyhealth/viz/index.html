Federated synthetic EHR — fidelity vs downstream utility

Four training regimes on the same 8-hospital eICU cohort, ordered by how much cross-site averaging they do. Prevalence fidelity on one axis, rare-code TSTR on the other.

Read the TSTR axis with suspicion. Every arm scores below chance on AUROC (0.5) and at or below the prior floor on recall@10 — a model that is inverted, not merely weak. The likely cause is a masking artifact: patients carrying more rare codes have more stripped from their input, so the model reads them as low-risk. Until that is resolved, the vertical axis measures a downstream task that is not working.
The axes are not matched the same way. Prevalence is capped at 2,000 synthetic patients per hospital for every regime. TSTR is uncapped, so centralized and fedavg trained on 16,000 records against local and fedavg_ft's 2,000. Part of the vertical spread is data volume, not generator quality.
