These runs are not comparable to the fidelity/utility board. Every number there was produced at dropout 0 with no latent; these change the training objective's stochasticity, so they get their own page and their own baseline (control, the dashed line: plain FedAvg at identical settings).

Best-of-K needs something to select over. At dropout 0 with no latent the K passes are bit-identical and best-of-K is best-of-1 at K× the cost — so a K>1 point is only meaningful where its row has a real source of spread. In the dropout panel that source is dropout; in the latent panel it is z, redrawn per candidate.
